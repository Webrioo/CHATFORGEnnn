Can you add an AI assistant to my existing website?

Yes. A business assistant can be built for your website and configured around the information you provide. The exact integration depends on your website platform and requirements.

Can the assistant use my business documents and data?

Yes, subject to the chosen setup. You can provide approved materials such as FAQs, product information, policies, and documents so the assistant can use that knowledge when responding.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. Start by explaining what you want the assistant to do. We’ll help turn that into a practical plan and explain any information or access needed.

Can I choose how my AI assistant behaves?

Yes. Its purpose, tone, instructions, and boundaries can be tailored to the use case. We’ll agree on what it should—and should not—help with.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the assistant’s complexity, integrations, knowledge sources, and ongoing needs. Tell us what you have in mind and we can discuss a suitable scope.
